    About tips

    Does support give me a badge or faster support?

    No. Tips buy no digital benefit, priority support, service, ownership, royalties, or revenue share. Optional website acknowledgement is a separate, freely withdrawable choice.

    What happens when I click Ko-fi?

    You leave this site for Ko-fi. Ko-fi and its processors handle checkout and their own cookies, personal data, fees and terms. Peersus never receives your card number. Nothing loads from a payment provider before you choose the link.

    Can I help without paying?

    Absolutely. Reproduce a problem, describe your device and steps, improve documentation, or help someone get started. Remove private paths, device identifiers, logs, and files before sharing a report as an issue on GitHub.
