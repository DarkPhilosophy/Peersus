PEERSUS · RELAY

peersus.axxa.dev is the Peersus relay.

Peersus moves files directly between your own devices. When two devices cannot reach each other directly, this server passes their encrypted traffic along, and nothing more.

What this server does

  • It helps two Peersus devices find each other and, if no direct path opens, forwards the traffic between them.
  • Peersus always tries a direct connection first; the relay is only the fallback.
  • It is an iroh relay, the open networking layer Peersus is built on.

What it cannot do

  • Read your files: transfers are end-to-end encrypted between your devices, and the relay never holds a key.
  • Store anything: data passes through and is not kept.
  • Know your contents. What it does see is connection metadata, such as which device addresses talk and when.

Using it

Nothing to set up: Peersus lists this relay by default, next to the public n0 relays. In Settings → Network → Relay you can switch it off, change the order relays are tried in, or add a relay of your own.
