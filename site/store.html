    How the store works

    This page only lists what was approved; it cannot approve or check anything. Peersus verifies the maintainer’s signature with its built-in key, refuses an archive whose size or SHA-256 differs from the signed entry, and installs a new extension with every permission refused. Updating forgets what the old version was allowed to do.

    Download is there to read what you would install. An archive installed by hand is treated as coming from outside the store, with its own warning. To compare a download with its signed digest:

    sha256sum <archive>

    What the project is building

    Four extensions are written by the project. They are described here so you know what to expect. None of them is in the store yet, and nothing on this site installs them.

    • Monitor

      How this device is doing: processor, graphics, memory, storage, network and battery, each shown only if you allow it. Numbers from another device are not shown yet.

      NOT IN THE STORE YET

    • Control

      Use your phone as a touchpad and keyboard for a paired computer. Both devices have to allow it.

      NOT IN THE STORE YET

    • Clipboard

      Share text between paired devices, by hand or, if you allow it, automatically. Android only lets an app read the clipboard while it is in front.

      NOT IN THE STORE YET · UNDER DEVELOPMENT

    • Upload

      Upload one file you choose, to a destination you approve. A provider may keep a readable copy: this is not the end-to-end encrypted transfer.
